These three tools solve the same frustration: you find a video worth saving, but the platform either hides the download button or slaps a watermark on it. Rex cuts the process down to paste-link-and-download.

  • YouTube Download: Video and audio extraction, fast and stable. Paste a link to grab 4K/8K HDR video or audio-only tracks. Supports Shorts and playlists, auto-merges audio, zero ads.
    Direct link: https://tool.rexai.top/youtube External link

  • Instagram Download: Save public photos, videos, and stories. Grabs Reels, IGTV, Stories, and multi-image posts in batch. Prioritizes watermark-free output. No Instagram login needed.

Content Creation Tools#

If you’ve ever tried pasting Markdown into the WeChat editor and watched your formatting disintegrate, these tools are for you.

    Direct link: https://tool.rexai.top/tools/wechat-markdown External link

  • WeChat Image Extractor: Extract and deduplicate images from WeChat articles, with batch download support. Paste an article link and grab every image at once — duplicates removed automatically.
    Direct link: https://tool.rexai.top/tools/wechat-image-extract External link
