GPT-5.2 Is Here! OpenAI Strikes Back at Gemini with Mind-Blowing Coding Skills

OpenAI releases GPT-5.2 triple threat - coding benchmarks shattered, 11x faster than human experts at 1% of the cost, Altman expects red alert lifted by January

December 12, 2024 · 4 min · 704 words · Rexai Programming -

Dude, the AI world just exploded again!

Remember how cocky Google was when they dropped Gemini 3 a few days ago? OpenAI literally went into red alert mode over there. Well, guess what - early this morning OpenAI dropped a bombshell: GPT-5.2 series is officially here!

Triple Threat Release#

OpenAI released three versions at once, kind of like Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers in a video game:

  • GPT-5.2 Instant: Your everyday quick assistant, fast responses, great for simple tasks
  • GPT-5.2 Pro: Officially “the smartest and most reliable” version, built for tough problems
  • GPT-5.2 Thinking: A reasoning beast, perfect for researchers and scientists

Basically, they’re doing tiered pricing so you can choose based on your needs. Don’t want to burn money? Use Instant. Got a complex problem? Bring out the Pro or Thinking heavy artillery.

Coding Capabilities Are Insane#

Honestly, as someone who writes code every day, what I care about most is whether it can actually code. The numbers came out and my jaw dropped:

  • SWE-Bench Pro test: 55.6%
  • SWE-Bench Verified test: shot up to 80%, breaking all previous records

What does this mean? It’s like AI coding went from being a fresh intern to a seasoned developer with 3 years of experience. Still can’t completely replace humans, but it can now independently handle a lot of complex tasks.

11x Faster Than Experts, 99% Cheaper#

This data seriously made me question reality.

OpenAI ran a test pitting GPT-5.2 against human experts on the same tasks:

MetricGPT-5.2Human Expert
Reaching expert level70.9%-
Completion speed11x1x
CostLess than 1%100%

Think about it - hiring an expert might cost hundreds per hour, while AI finishes in a minute for pennies. Of course, this doesn’t mean experts are out of jobs - it means AI can now handle tons of repetitive professional work for you.

It’s like how you used to wash clothes by hand, but now you have a washing machine. The washing machine can’t completely replace you (someone still needs to put the clothes in, right?), but it definitely frees up a ton of your time.

Long-Context Processing Is Also Strong#

These days, lots of people use AI to process long documents - analyzing contracts, summarizing reports, that kind of thing. GPT-5.2 has improved here too:

Within a 256K token range, accuracy approaches 100%.

What’s 256K tokens? Roughly equivalent to a 300-400 page book. This means you can throw an entire technical manual at it, ask it to find key information, and it basically won’t miss anything.

Altman Says Red Alert Lifted by January#

After Gemini 3 dropped, rumor has it OpenAI went into red alert internally. But CEO Altman came out today and said:

“Gemini 3’s impact on our metrics probably wasn’t as big as we feared”

Translation: We’re ready, not worried. Expects to lift the red alert before January next year.

Honestly, the AI war is getting more intense. Google, OpenAI, Anthropic - they’re all chasing each other, and the real winners are us users. Models keep getting stronger, prices keep dropping.

API Pricing#

Speaking of prices, here’s the GPT-5.2 API pricing:

  • Input: $1.75 / million tokens
  • Output: $14 / million tokens
  • Cached input gets 90% discount

How do I feel about this pricing? It’s a bit more expensive than previous models, but considering the capability boost, the value is still pretty solid. Especially that cache discount - if your app has lots of repeated inputs (like system prompts), you can save a bunch.

My Take#

As a developer who uses AI-assisted coding every single day, I think this GPT-5.2 upgrade is genuinely substantial. The coding capability improvements are real - not one of those “benchmark gaming but meh in practice” fake upgrades.

That said, no matter how powerful AI gets, it’s still just a tool. Like how having a calculator doesn’t mean you can skip learning math, having AI write code doesn’t mean you can ignore code logic. Its biggest value is helping you be more efficient, not replacing your thinking.

Alright, that’s it for today. I’m gonna go try out the new model - I’ll let you know if I find anything interesting!
