Frequently Asked Questions

My box only has C++ llama.cpp. Can I deploy DSpark?

Yes. llama.cpp carries native draft-dspark speculative decoding since 2026-07 (PR #25173). Attach one 1.5G DSpark Q8 sidecar GGUF and one --spec-type draft-dspark flag turns it on. No vLLM, no Python.

Does 32G hold it?

Yes. Main model Q4_K_M is about 17.2G + the DSpark Q8 sidecar about 1.5G, total about 18.7G, leaving about 13G for context. FP16 full-precision 54G will not fit one card; run the Q4 tier. Figures are size-based estimates, not measured on your box.

DSpark or the built-in MTP?

DSpark bets deeper (up to 7 tokens per round) and is fastest on structured work - short code, short QA, math (single-card community numbers 1.7 to 2.5x). Built-in MTP is zero-download, better for open-ended long-form, and holds up better at high concurrency. Pick by what you actually do.

What are the gotchas?

Two: (1) write both -ngl 99 and -ngld 99 - a sidecar left on the CPU erases the speedup; (2) use --spec-draft-n-max 4 (the measured C++ peak), not 7 (that is the vLLM number).
